Women Safety & Crime Trends in India (2001–2022)

Project Overview-

This project analyzes crimes against women in India from 2001 to 2022 using official government data. The objective is to identify long-term trends, major crime categories, and key insights through interactive dashboards.The analysis is performed using Power BI and enhanced with Generative AI tools for insight validation, documentation, and workflow optimization.

⚠️ Note: Although it is 2026, publicly available and machine-readable government datasets are currently available only up to 2022. Later data exists in annual reports but is not widely released in CSV format.

🎯 Objectives-

-Analyze long-term crime trends against women -Identify major crime categories -Understand year-wise growth patterns -Create interactive and user-friendly dashboards -Apply AI-assisted analytics for better insights

📂 Dataset-

-Source: National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB) / data.gov.in -Time Period: 2001 – 2022 -Format: CSV -Type: Public Government Dataset

🛠 Tools & Technologies-

-Power BI -Microsoft Excel -Power Query -DAX -Generative AI (for analysis support and documentation)

📊 Dashboards Included

Women Safety & Crime Trends Dashboard-

-Total Crimes Reported -Peak Crime Year -Crimes by Type -Top 5 Crimes -Year-wise Trends -Interactive Filters

Rape Cases Analysis Dashboard-

-Total Rape Cases -Peak Year -Year-wise Comparison -Trend Analysis -Forecast Visualization

Key Insights-

-Over 6 million crime cases were reported between 2001–2022 -The highest number of cases was recorded in 2022 -The most reported category is Cruelty by Husband/Relatives -Rape cases show a steady increase over the years -Data visualization helps identify hidden patterns

Skills Demonstrated-

-Data Cleaning & Preparation -Data Modeling -Time-Series Analysis -Dashboard Design -Data Storytelling -AI-Assisted Analytics
